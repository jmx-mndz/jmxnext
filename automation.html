WORKFLOW 01 · LEAD NURTURE

New Lead Nurture — Claim Offer

Built for speed-to-lead. The moment someone submits a Facebook lead form, they're in the pipeline and hearing from the business — and the workflow decides what happens next based on whether, and how, they reply.

  • Trigger: Facebook lead form submitted
  • Opportunity created in New Lead; contact added to long-term nurture
  • Conversational email + SMS go out instantly
  • Waits for a reply (moves on after 2 minutes)
  • Replied: moved to Hot Lead — positive replies get a booking link, negative ones a survey link
  • No reply: Call Connect rings the lead, then an "any questions?" SMS the next day

Handoff: when a nurtured lead books, Workflow 02 takes over and removes them from the nurture sequence.
WORKFLOW 02 · BOOKINGS

Appointment Confirmation + Reminders

Once a lead books, sales follow-ups stop and the reminders start. The deal moves to Booking automatically, and the customer hears from the business at exactly the right moments before they show up.

Appointment confirmed→ Removed from nurture→ Opportunity → Booking→ Confirmation email→ 24 hr reminder email→ 1 hr email + SMS

WORKFLOW 03 · LEAD ROUTING

Buyer / Seller Automation — Real Estate

One intake form that sorts real estate leads into buyers and sellers, tags and follows up with each, and alerts the agent the moment a lead is worth dropping everything for.

  • Trigger: form submitted → contact and opportunity created
  • Buyers: tagged, emailed and texted; a $250K–$500K budget flags a high-budget lead, alerts the agent and hands off to Conversation AI
  • Sellers: tagged, emailed and texted; "as soon as possible" sellers are flagged urgent with email + SMS alerts to the team
  • Neither: tagged Needs Review with an internal alert

SCENARIO 01 · CONTENT OPS

Content Calendar → Social Publishing

Turns an Airtable content calendar into scheduled posts. When a record is ready, Make queues it in Buffer, then splits into two routes: one writes the result back to Airtable, the other tells the team on Slack.

AirtableBufferRouterIteratorSlack
Main path
  • Airtable watches the content calendar on a schedule
  • Filter: only records with a link post continue
  • Buffer creates the status update
  • Router splits into two routes
Routes
  • Route 1: Iterator + text aggregator compile the post details, then Airtable updates the record
  • Route 2: Slack posts a message so the team knows it's queued
