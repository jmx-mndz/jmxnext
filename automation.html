A custom-coded funnel reviewers can verify in seconds.

Home, Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages, with the booking calendar embedded in the request form. Customers click Request a Repair, pick a slot, then complete their details and consent, all on one page.

  • Unchecked SMS consent box, voluntary and never a condition of purchase
  • Program details: who texts, message types, frequency, "msg & data rates may apply"

  • Privacy Policy and Terms linked at the point of consent and in the footer

  • Clear service description that matches the campaign use case

Meet Joe: one assistant for chat and phone.

Joe greets the customer, asks the appliance and brand, states the $70 service call fee, collects the address and phone, then offers the single nearest 3-hour window from the live calendar.

  • One question at a time, never asks for info already given
  • Writes appliance, brand, address, ZIP and phone to custom fields
  • Books only with complete details and real availability
  • Auto-pilot with 8-second reply spacing so it feels natural
  • Pauses when a team member steps in, resumes after 1 hour
  • Matching Voice AI agent with a natural, filler-word voice

From first click to booked appointment.

Workflows that catch a lead the moment it lands, follow up until it books, and keep the pipeline honest along the way.

Workflow 01 · Lead nurture

New Lead Nurture — Claim Offer

Built for speed-to-lead. The moment someone submits a Facebook lead form, they're in the pipeline and hearing from the business — and the workflow decides what happens next based on whether, and how, they reply.

  1. Trigger: Facebook lead form submitted
  2. Opportunity created in New Lead; contact added to long-term nurture
  3. Conversational email + SMS go out instantly
  4. Waits for a reply (moves on after 2 minutes)
  5. Replied: moved to Hot Lead — positive replies get a booking link, negative ones a survey link
  6. No reply: Call Connect rings the lead, then an "any questions?" SMS the next day

Workflow 03 · Lead routing

Buyer / Seller Automation — Real Estate

One intake form that sorts real estate leads into buyers and sellers, tags and follows up with each, and alerts the agent the moment a lead is worth dropping everything for.

  1. Trigger: form submitted → contact and opportunity created
  2. Buyers: tagged, emailed and texted; a $250K–$500K budget flags a high-budget lead, alerts the agent and hands off to Conversation AI
  3. Sellers: tagged, emailed and texted; "as soon as possible" sellers are flagged urgent with email + SMS alerts to the team
  4. Neither: tagged Needs Review with an internal alert

Scenario 01 · Content ops

Content Calendar → Social Publishing

Turns an Airtable content calendar into scheduled posts. When a record is ready, Make queues it in Buffer, then splits into two routes: one writes the result back to Airtable, the other tells the team on Slack.

Main path
  1. Airtable watches the content calendar on a schedule
  2. Filter: only records with a link post continue
  3. Buffer creates the status update
  4. Router splits into two routes
Routes
  1. Route 1: Iterator + text aggregator compile the post details, then Airtable updates the record
  2. Route 2: Slack posts a message so the team knows it's queued
